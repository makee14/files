### Task 2 — Verdict
- **Verdict**: HIGHLY USABLE
- **Why this verdict + recency status**: The 2025 source passes recency and reports four grounded mechanisms across four allowed 1.2 slots: database-checked identity gating, owner slot reservation, distinct role access, and a comparative security boundary.
- **Slots populated → Features 1–7 mapping**: Primary-Intervention-Mechanism, Function-Feature-1-7, Role-Gated-Value-Creation, and System-Novelty map to Features 1 and 2; security is treated as an internal mechanism of those features, not a separate feature.
- **Missing slots / metrics**: Metric-Change-Target remains unpopulated because the source reports functional and 31-participant acceptance results but no baseline-to-outcome change, latency, booking-completion, or breach-rate metric.
- **Panel use**: The citation can support the claim that an EHR and appointment portal require explicit identity, permission, and available-slot mechanisms rather than relying on feature labels alone.
- **Transfer risk / caveat**: DVAS includes a pharmacist role, which is outside the locked CarePaws personas; the transfer therefore retains only veterinarian/administrator, staff/receptionist, and pet-owner access boundaries.
