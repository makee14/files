### Task 2 — Verdict
- **Verdict**: HIGHLY USABLE (4 mechanisms, 4 slots)
- **Bullets (3–5)**:
  - **Why this verdict + recency status**: PASS (2016). The authors developed and tested a 21-field XML schema, achieved complete extraction in a test PMS, and validated a 10% real-practice sample against printed records.
  - **Slots populated → Features 1–7 mapping**: [Primary-Intervention-Mechanism], [Function-Feature-1-7], [Metric-Change-Target], and [System-Novelty] → Feature 2. No transfer is made to Features 1, 3–7.
  - **Missing slots / metrics this source cannot cover**: [Role-Gated-Value-Creation] and CarePaws-specific role/task timings are not established; cross-PMS portability was proposed rather than tested.
  - **Panel use**: deflects “a digital record is merely storage” by supplying a structured-field mechanism and empirical completeness/accuracy evidence; it also constrains an inflated first-claim defense.
  - **Transfer risk / caveat**: the pilot used one mixed-animal PMS and a UK practice, whereas CarePaws is canine/feline only at one Philippine clinic; research extraction must not become an eighth feature.
- **Transfer boundary**: CarePaws may claim only the structured clinical-record and integrity mechanism within Feature 2—not the source’s research-extraction, surveillance, or cross-vendor capability.
