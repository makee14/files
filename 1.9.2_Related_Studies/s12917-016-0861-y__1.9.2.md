## Task 2 — Verdict

- **Verdict**: HIGHLY USABLE (4 mechanisms across 4 allowed slots)
- **Why this verdict and recency status**: PASS (2016); the schema was tested in a test PMS and a real veterinary practice with record-level validation.
- **Slots populated → Features 1–7 mapping**: [Empirical-System-Architecture], [Connectivity-Assumptions], [Quantitative-Metric-Outcomes], [Acknowledged-Architectural-Flaws] → Features 1 and 2.
- **Missing evidence**: No hardware/offline fallback, response latency, interface acceptance, or comparative practice-performance outcome.
- **Panel use**: Deflects the claim that veterinary EHR exports are unvalidated across heterogeneous practice systems.
- **Transfer caveat**: This validates research-data extraction, not clinical workflow or diagnostic completeness; only 6% of visits had diagnostic codes.
