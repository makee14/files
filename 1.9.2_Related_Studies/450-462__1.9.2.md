## Task 2 — Verdict

- **Verdict**: HIGHLY USABLE
- **Recency and evidence**: PASS; the 2025 prototype reports four relevant mechanisms spanning four allowed 1.9.2 slots, each grounded in the source.
- **Slots populated → Features 1–7 mapping**: [Empirical-System-Architecture], [Connectivity-Assumptions], [Quantitative-Metric-Outcomes], and [Acknowledged-Architectural-Flaws] → Features 1, 2, 3, and 6.
- **Missing evidence**: No database engine, offline fallback, response-latency measurement, SUS score, score dispersion, or comparison-group outcome is reported.
- **Panel use**: This citation deflects the claim that veterinary prototypes lack role-segregated architecture or post-build testing, while also showing why interface acceptance alone cannot substantiate system performance.
- **Transfer caveat**: The source’s eight-digit email OTP mechanism is reported as a feature, but CarePaws must not treat acceptance of that mechanism as proof of offline resilience, latency, or clinical-record safety.
