### Task 2 — Verdict
- **Verdict**: HIGHLY USABLE (4 mechanisms, 4 slots)
- **Bullets**:
  - Why + recency: PASS (2025); the source states four usable objective mechanisms spanning system development, staff requirements elicitation, veterinarian defect testing, and quantitative UX evaluation.
  - Slots populated → Features: [General-Objective-Action] → Features 2, 3; [Phase1-QUAL-Requirements-Objective] → Feature 2; [Phase3-Technical-Verification-Objective] → Features 1, 2, 3; [Phase4-QUAN-Evaluation-Objective] → Features 1, 2, 3.
  - Missing: [Phase2-Agile-Engineering-Objective] because the source explicitly used Waterfall; no direct objective mechanism maps to Features 4–7, and no clinic-specific threshold is established.
  - Panel use: Deflects “objectives are generic and unmeasured” by pairing develop/interview/test/evaluate verbs with an n=102 UX survey and an at-least-95% excellent-rating result.
  - Caveat: Its adoption module is out of scope, and its Results claim of at least 95% excellent efficiency ratings conflicts with its Discussion statement that many respondents disagreed or strongly disagreed about task efficiency.
