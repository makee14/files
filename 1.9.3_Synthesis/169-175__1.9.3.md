## Task 2 — Verdict

- **Verdict**: HIGHLY USABLE (4 gap mechanisms across 4 slots)
- **Recency and evidential basis**: PASS; the source reports a Waterfall-built veterinary web system, post-build testing, and a survey of 102 respondents in 2025.
- **Slots populated → Features**: Methodological-Gap, Geographical-Gap, Architectural-Gap, and Empirical-Gap → Features 1, 2, 3, and 4.
- **Missing evidence**: Scale bounds, item wording, reliability, respondent-stratum counts, response rate, item-level efficiency counts, SD, significance tests, latency, uptime, and Philippine/Urdaneta validation were not stated; CarePaws-Synthesis-Resolution must remain author synthesis.
- **Panel use**: This citation deflects the claim that a modern, sizable, post-build UX evaluation automatically establishes task effectiveness: the aggregate efficiency claim conflicts with the discussion reporting many negative efficiency responses.
- **Transfer caveat**: Pet-adoption capability is outside Features 1–7; only the source's gap evidence and transferable evaluation/architecture implications are used.
